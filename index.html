Who We Are

We Handle the Work Behind the Work

Attorneys spend too much time on tasks that don't require a J.D. We handle those tasks — accurately and on deadline — so your team stays focused on what actually moves cases forward.

Legally Trained Staff

Our paralegals understand how U.S. law firms operate. They're not generalists — they know court filing rules, discovery conventions, and how attorneys prefer work product delivered.

Fraction of In-House Cost

Hiring a full-time paralegal in the U.S. averages $55,000–$75,000 a year before benefits. Our model gives you the same output at a fraction of that — no benefits, no desk space, no HR overhead.

NDA-Protected, Always

Every engagement is covered by a mutual NDA before work begins. Client information, case details, and documents stay strictly within your firm and our assigned team — no exceptions.
